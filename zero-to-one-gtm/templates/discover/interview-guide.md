# Interview guide

Ask for the last time. Do not pitch, and do not ask whether they would use the product.

- Walk me through the last time this happened.
- What triggered it?
- How did you handle it?
- What did that cost in time, money, risk, or lost opportunity?
- What have you already tried?
- Who cared, who approved, and who did the work?
- Where did you look for an answer?
- What happened when you did nothing?

Close by asking who else deals with this regularly.
