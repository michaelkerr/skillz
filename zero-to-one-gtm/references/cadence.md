# Weekly cadence

About three to four hours. The rest of the week is for building.

| Day | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Evidence review. Who engaged, what language landed, what fell flat. Update the ledger. | 30 min |
| Tuesday | Prepare one thing to share, or design the single riskiest experiment. Set the audience, action, metric, threshold, duration, and decision rule before any outreach. | 45 min |
| Wednesday | Post in the two or three chosen channels. Respond to open threads. | 30 min |
| Thursday | Follow up within 24 hours of every meaningful conversation. Thank them, request one introduction, schedule the next conversation. | 30 min |
| Friday | Have the conversations. Take notes. Ask for an introduction at the end of each one. Then synthesize: one decision. | 1–2 hours |

Always: every promising interaction ends with a dated next step. After a demonstrated win, ask for a relevant introduction.

## What normal looks like

| When | Expected | Red flag |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Channels chosen. First artifact shared. Possibly no engagement. | — |
| Week 2 | A few reactions. Maybe one DM. | — |
| Weeks 3–4 | Two to five real conversations. Repeated language starts. | Zero engagement after three posts. Reassess the channel or the problem framing. |
| Weeks 5–8 | Five to fifteen conversations. Introductions start. One framing pulls ahead. | Conversations happen and nobody describes real pain. The problem may be milder than assumed. |
| Weeks 8–12 | A small group engages repeatedly. ICP and offer can sharpen. Design-partner talks are natural. | Many conversations and no cluster. The audience is too broad, or the problem is not consequential enough. |

Silence in week one is normal. The introduction flywheel shows up around weeks four to six.

## Dual track

| Product track | GTM track | Shared decision |
|---|---|---|
| Prototype a capability | Test whether the problem and outcome matter | Continue, narrow, or stop |
| Build the minimum workflow | Recruit qualified design partners or first users | Which workflow deserves investment |
| Instrument the core action | Define the first-value event | Does behavior indicate value |
| Fix friction | Observe onboarding and objections | Product issue, message issue, or customer mismatch |
| Release an improvement | Retest offer and activation | Did the change alter customer behavior |
